# 11. Matnli masalani tenglamaga aylantirish

*SAT bo'limi:* Algebra

## Bu bobda

9-bobda tenglamalarni yechishni o'rgandik. Lekin hayotda (va SAT'da) tenglama tayyor holda berilmaydi: avval uni so'zlardan o'zingiz tuzib olishingiz kerak. Ko'p o'quvchilar uchun eng qiyin qadam aynan shu. Bu bobda:

- "3 ga ko'p", "2 marta kam", "yig'indining yarmi" kabi so'zlarni algebraik ifodaga aylantirishni;
- matnli masalani yechishning to'rt qadamli usulini;
- ketma-ket sonlar, yosh, pul, tezlik–masofa–vaqt va aralashma masalalarini;
- topilgan javobni masala shartiga qo'yib tekshirishni o'rganamiz.

**SAT'da qayerda kerak?** SAT Algebra bo'limidagi savollarning katta qismi matnli: "Which equation represents this situation?", "How many adult tickets were sold?" Ularni yechish uchun ingliz tilidagi matnni tenglamaga aylantira bilish kerak. Shuning uchun bu bobda o'zbekcha iboralar yonida inglizchasini ham beramiz.

## Tushuntirish

### So'zlardan ifodaga

*Matnli masala (word problem)* — so'zlar bilan aytilgan masala. Uni yechish uchun har bir iborani matematik belgilarga "tarjima" qilamiz. Quyidagi jadvalda $x$ — biror noma'lum son.

| O'zbekcha | English | Ifoda |
|---|---|---|
| $x$ dan $3$ ga ko'p | $3$ more than $x$ | $x + 3$ |
| $x$ dan $3$ ga kam | $3$ less than $x$ | $x - 3$ |
| $x$ ning ikki baravari, $x$ dan $2$ marta ko'p | twice $x$ | $2x$ |
| $x$ dan $3$ marta ko'p | $3$ times $x$ | $3x$ |
| $x$ ning yarmi, $x$ dan $2$ marta kam | half of $x$ | $\frac{x}{2}$ |
| $x$ va $y$ ning yig'indisi | the sum of $x$ and $y$ | $x + y$ |
| $x$ va $y$ ning ayirmasi | the difference of $x$ and $y$ | $x - y$ |
| $x$ va $y$ ning ko'paytmasi | the product of $x$ and $y$ | $xy$ |
| $x$ ning $20\%$ i | $20\%$ of $x$ | $0.2x$ |
| $x$ ning kvadrati | the square of $x$ | $x^2$ |

"Teng", "bo'ladi", "chiqadi", "hosil bo'ladi" (inglizchada *is*, *equals*, *results in*) so'zlari tenglik belgisiga aylanadi.

Ikki narsaga alohida e'tibor bering.

- **Tartib.** "$x$ dan $3$ ga kam" — bu $x - 3$, $3 - x$ emas. Inglizchada ham "$3$ less than $x$" — $x - 3$: so'zlar tartibi teskari, ehtiyot bo'ling.
- **Qavs.** "Yig'indining ikki baravari" — $2(x + y)$, "ikki baravarining yig'indisi" esa boshqa narsa. "$x$ ning ikki baravaridan $5$ ga kam" — $2x - 5$, "$x$ dan $5$ ga kam sonning ikki baravari" esa $2(x - 5)$.

Shubha bo'lsa, $x$ o'rniga aniq son qo'yib tekshiring: "$10$ dan $3$ ga kam" — $7$, ya'ni $10 - 3$. Demak, "$x$ dan $3$ ga kam" — $x - 3$.

### To'rt qadamli usul

1. **Belgilang.** Masalani oxirigacha o'qing: nima so'ralmoqda? Noma'lum miqdorni harf bilan belgilang va buni yozib qo'ying: "$x$ — kichik o'g'ilning yoshi". Odatda qolgan miqdorlar u orqali oson ifodalanadigan miqdorni tanlang (ko'pincha eng kichigini).
2. **Ifodalang.** Masaladagi boshqa miqdorlarni shu harf orqali yozing.
3. **Tenglama tuzing.** Matnda ikki xil yo'l bilan aytilgan bitta miqdorni toping ("jami 85 ta", "yoshlari teng bo'ladi") va ikkalasini tenglang.
4. **Yeching va tekshiring.** Tenglamani yeching (9-bob). Javobni **matn shartiga** qo'yib tekshiring (tenglamaga emas: tenglama noto'g'ri tuzilgan bo'lishi mumkin). Savol aynan nimani so'raganiga qarang: $x$ ni emas, boshqa miqdorni so'ragan bo'lishi mumkin.

::: {.misol}
Bir sonning uch baravaridan $7$ ni ayirsak, shu sonning o'zidan $5$ ga ko'p son hosil bo'ladi. Sonni toping.

*Yechish.*

1-qadam: $x$ — izlanayotgan son.

2-qadam: "sonning uch baravaridan $7$ ni ayirsak" — $3x - 7$; "sonning o'zidan $5$ ga ko'p son" — $x + 5$.

3-qadam: "hosil bo'ladi" — tenglik: $3x - 7 = x + 5$.

4-qadam: $3x - x = 5 + 7$, $2x = 12$, $x = 6$.

*Tekshirish (matn bo'yicha):* $6$ ning uch baravari $18$, undan $7$ ni ayirsak $11$. $6$ dan $5$ ga ko'p son ham $11$. To'g'ri.

*Javob:* $6$.
:::

### Ketma-ket sonlar

Ketma-ket kelgan butun sonlar bir-biridan $1$ ga farq qiladi: $7, 8, 9$. Shuning uchun *ketma-ket butun sonlar (consecutive integers)* $n$, $n + 1$, $n + 2$, ... deb belgilanadi.

*Ketma-ket juft sonlar (consecutive even integers)*, masalan $10, 12, 14$, bir-biridan $2$ ga farq qiladi: $n$, $n + 2$, $n + 4$ ($n$ juft). *Ketma-ket toq sonlar (consecutive odd integers)*, masalan $7, 9, 11$, **ham** bir-biridan $2$ ga farq qiladi: $n$, $n + 2$, $n + 4$ ($n$ toq). Toq sonlar uchun $n + 1$, $n + 3$ yozish xato: $n$ toq bo'lsa, $n + 1$ juft bo'ladi.

::: {.misol}
Uchta ketma-ket toq sonning yig'indisi $51$ ga teng. Ularning eng kattasini toping.

*Yechish.* $n$ — eng kichik son. Unda sonlar $n$, $n + 2$, $n + 4$. Yig'indisi:

$$n + (n + 2) + (n + 4) = 51, \qquad 3n + 6 = 51, \qquad 3n = 45, \qquad n = 15.$$

Sonlar: $15$, $17$, $19$. Savol eng kattasini so'rayapti: $19$.

*Tekshirish:* $15 + 17 + 19 = 51$, uchalasi ham toq va ketma-ket.

*Javob:* $19$.
:::

### Yosh masalalari

Yosh masalalarida vaqt o'tishini jadval bilan kuzatish qulay. Asosiy qoida: $k$ yildan keyin **har bir** odamning yoshiga $k$ qo'shiladi, $k$ yil oldin esa har biridan $k$ ayiriladi. Ikki odamning yoshlari farqi esa hech qachon o'zgarmaydi.

::: {.misol}
Ota hozir o'g'lidan $3$ marta katta. $12$ yildan keyin ota o'g'lidan $2$ marta katta bo'ladi. O'g'il hozir necha yoshda?

*Yechish.* $x$ — o'g'ilning hozirgi yoshi. Jadval tuzamiz:

| | Hozir | $12$ yildan keyin |
|---|---|---|
| O'g'il | $x$ | $x + 12$ |
| Ota | $3x$ | $3x + 12$ |

$12$ yildan keyin otaning yoshi o'g'linikidan $2$ marta katta:

$$3x + 12 = 2(x + 12), \qquad 3x + 12 = 2x + 24, \qquad x = 12.$$

*Tekshirish:* hozir o'g'il $12$ yoshda, ota $36$ yoshda ($3$ marta katta). $12$ yildan keyin: $24$ va $48$, ya'ni $2$ marta katta. To'g'ri.

*Javob:* $12$ yoshda.
:::

### Pul masalalari

Pul masalalarida ikki xil tenglik bo'ladi: **narsalar soni** bo'yicha va **pul miqdori** bo'yicha. Agar jami $28$ ta narsa bo'lsa va ulardan $x$ tasi bir turdan bo'lsa, qolganlari $28 - x$ ta. Pul miqdori esa har bir tur uchun "narsalar soni marta bittasining narxi" ko'paytmalarining yig'indisi.

::: {.misol}
Kassada faqat $5000$ so'mlik va $10\,000$ so'mlik pullar bor, hammasi $28$ ta, umumiy summasi esa $195\,000$ so'm. Kassada nechta $10\,000$ so'mlik pul bor?

*Yechish.* $x$ — $10\,000$ so'mliklar soni. Unda $5000$ so'mliklar soni $28 - x$. Umumiy summa:

$$10\,000x + 5000(28 - x) = 195\,000.$$

Qavsni ochamiz: $10\,000x + 140\,000 - 5000x = 195\,000$, ya'ni $5000x = 55\,000$, $x = 11$.

*Tekshirish:* $11$ ta $10\,000$ so'mlik — $110\,000$ so'm; $28 - 11 = 17$ ta $5000$ so'mlik — $85\,000$ so'm. Jami $195\,000$ so'm, pullar soni $28$ ta. To'g'ri.

*Javob:* $11$ ta.
:::

### Tezlik, masofa, vaqt

5-bobdan bilamiz: *masofa (distance)* tezlik bilan *vaqt (time)* ko'paytmasiga teng, $d = vt$. Ikki jism harakatlanadigan masalalarda:

- bir-biriga qarab (yoki qarama-qarshi tomonga) harakatlansa, ular orasidagi masofa har soatda tezliklar **yig'indisi** qadar o'zgaradi;
- bir tomonga harakatlanib, biri ikkinchisini quvib yetsa, ular orasidagi masofa har soatda tezliklar **ayirmasi** qadar qisqaradi.

::: {.misol}
Ikki shahar orasidagi masofa $350$ km. Bir vaqtda bir shahardan avtomobil soatiga $80$ km tezlikda, ikkinchi shahardan esa unga qarab avtobus soatiga $60$ km tezlikda yo'lga chiqdi. Necha soatdan keyin ular uchrashadi?

*Yechish.* $t$ — uchrashguncha o'tgan vaqt (soat). Shu vaqtda avtomobil $80t$ km, avtobus $60t$ km yuradi. Uchrashganda ular birgalikda butun yo'lni bosib o'tgan bo'ladi:

$$80t + 60t = 350, \qquad 140t = 350, \qquad t = 2.5.$$

*Tekshirish:* $2.5$ soatda avtomobil $200$ km, avtobus $150$ km yuradi; $200 + 150 = 350$. To'g'ri.

*Javob:* $2.5$ soatdan keyin (2 soat 30 daqiqa).
:::

### Aralashma masalalari

*Aralashma (mixture)* masalalarida "toza modda" miqdorini kuzatamiz. Masalan, tuzli suv — *eritma (solution)* da tuz miqdori eritma massasining foiziga teng (6-bob): $200$ g $20\%$ li eritmada $0.2 \cdot 200 = 40$ g tuz bor. Eritmaga toza suv qo'shilsa, tuz miqdori **o'zgarmaydi**, faqat eritma massasi ortadi.

Shu eritmaga necha gramm suv qo'shsak, $8\%$ li eritma chiqadi? $w$ — qo'shiladigan suv (g). Yangi massa $200 + w$, tuz esa o'sha $40$ g:

$$0.08(200 + w) = 40, \qquad 200 + w = 500, \qquad w = 300.$$

Narxlari har xil mahsulotlar aralashmasida ham xuddi shunday: aralashmaning umumiy narxi qismlar narxlarining yig'indisiga teng.

## Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

**1. "Ga kam" iborasida tartibni almashtirish.**

*Noto'g'ri:* "$y$ dan $8$ ga kam son" — $8 - y$. Inglizchada: "$8$ less than $y$" — $8 - y$.

*To'g'ri:* $y - 8$. Son bilan tekshiring: "$20$ dan $8$ ga kam" — $12 = 20 - 8$.

**2. "Marta ko'p" ni teskari yozish.**

*Noto'g'ri:* "Ali Validan $3$ marta ko'p olma terdi" — $3A = V$ ($A$ — Alining, $V$ — Valining olmalari soni).

*To'g'ri:* Alining olmasi ko'p, demak $A = 3V$. Son bilan tekshiring: Vali $10$ ta tersa, Ali $30$ ta teradi.

**3. Yosh masalasida yillarni faqat bitta odamga qo'shish.**

*Noto'g'ri:* Misol 3 da "$12$ yildan keyin" uchun $3x + 12 = 2x + 12$ yoki $3x = 2(x + 12)$.

*To'g'ri:* $12$ yil **ikkalasiga** ham qo'shiladi: $3x + 12 = 2(x + 12)$.

**4. Ketma-ket toq (juft) sonlarni $1$ qadam bilan yozish.**

*Noto'g'ri:* ketma-ket toq sonlar $n$, $n + 1$, $n + 2$.

*To'g'ri:* toq sonlar ham, juft sonlar ham $2$ qadam bilan keladi: $n$, $n + 2$, $n + 4$.

**5. Savolga emas, $x$ ga javob berish.**

*Noto'g'ri:* Misol 2 da javob $15$ deb yozish.

*To'g'ri:* $n = 15$ — eng kichik son, savol esa eng kattasini so'ragan: $19$. Javob yozishdan oldin savolni qayta o'qing.

## SAT ko'prigi

Quyidagi savollar SAT uslubida, inglizcha yozilgan. *Represents* — "ifodalaydi", *ticket* — chipta, *apart* — bir-biridan uzoqda. Javoblar kitob oxirida.

::: {.sat #s11-1 javob="B"}
Which expression represents $7$ less than $3$ times a number $n$?

A) $7 - 3n$

B) $3n - 7$

C) $3(n - 7)$

D) $3(7 - n)$
:::

::: {.sat #s11-2 javob="48"}
The sum of three consecutive integers is $141$. What is the greatest of the three integers?
:::

::: {.sat #s11-3 javob="90"}
A theater sold $150$ tickets for a total of \$1,560. Adult tickets cost \$12 each, and student tickets cost \$8 each. How many adult tickets did the theater sell?
:::

::: {.sat #s11-4 javob="A"}
Maria's age is $4$ years more than twice her brother's age. The sum of their ages is $31$ years. If $b$ represents her brother's age, in years, which equation represents this situation?

A) $b + (2b + 4) = 31$

B) $b + 2(b + 4) = 31$

C) $2b + 4 = 31$

D) $b + 4b + 2 = 31$
:::

::: {.sat #s11-5 javob="3"}
Two trains leave the same station at the same time and travel in opposite directions. One train travels at $70$ miles per hour, and the other travels at $50$ miles per hour. After how many hours will the trains be $360$ miles apart?
:::
